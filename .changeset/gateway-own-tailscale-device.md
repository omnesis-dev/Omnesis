A gateway whose installation includes its Tailscale helper program, as the Docker image does, can join your Tailscale network as a device of its own, with no Tailscale app on its machine: turn it on in **Settings → Tailscale**. It stays off, and writes nothing, until you do. Its public address for cloud agents is now chosen with the other addresses under **MCP server**.
