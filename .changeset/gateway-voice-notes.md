Tell Omnesis voice notes can be transcribed by the gateway's transcriber. While a transcriber is assigned and the new `inference.dictation.transcribeOnGateway` setting is on (the default; Settings → Models → Transcriber, or Settings → Voice in the apps), the iOS and Android apps record Tell Omnesis — including Apple Watch notes — as a voice note: the note is saved at once and the gateway replaces its text with its own transcript when ready. Watch notes wait on the watch until the iPhone receives them, and the watch returns to its face after sending. Omnesis Demo gains its own Apple Watch app.
