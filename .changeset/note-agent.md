The note agent acts on the Tell Omnesis notes you capture from the portal, the apps or the CLI whenever a `background-agent` model is assigned: one bounded turn per note adds, completes, moves or strikes the tasks it mentions, remembers facts you state about yourself or people you know, answers a question it asks, and leaves a receipt with Undo. A voice note waits for its transcript first. Notes from external agents are saved and searchable but never acted on. `agent.noteAgent: false` (Settings → Models → Background agent → Act on Tell Omnesis notes) switches it off without unassigning the model; notes captured while it is off are never acted on later.
