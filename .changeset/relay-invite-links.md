Omnesis accounts are invite-only. Creating an account needs an invite link: paste it under Settings → Connectivity → Omnesis account, or run `omnesis cloud connect --invite <link>`. Each invite creates one account and expires, and with an invite you can create your account with a passkey alone, without Google. Signing in to an account you already have needs no invite. Opening an invite link in a browser shows whether it still works.
