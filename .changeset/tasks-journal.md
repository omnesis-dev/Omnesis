Tasks: a list of what you mean to do, kept like a bullet journal, in the portal (`/portal/tasks`) and the iOS and Android apps, on every gateway with or without a model. Each task has a planned day, month or someday apart from any real deadline, and the Today, Month, Future, Anytime, Waiting and Done views show where it stands. Complete, move, strike or edit a task, see its history with where each change was made, and undo the latest change from its receipt. The agent gets task tools in every conversation, so a task can also change from a chat or a Tell Omnesis note.
