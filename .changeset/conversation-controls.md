Add clarification choices, queued follow-ups, interrupt-and-send corrections, saved drafts, and edit-and-resend to conversations in the portal, iOS, and Android. Accepted follow-ups and unanswered questions persist with the gateway conversation; drafts stay on the device. Queued follow-ups accumulate into one message; Send now interrupts the current turn and delivers that message. Message context menus offer Copy, and user messages offer Edit and resend. Edits append a new prompt and preserve the original exchange. New mobile apps retain ordinary chat with older gateways, and older apps can answer clarification questions in text.
