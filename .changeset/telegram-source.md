Experimental Telegram source, offered when the gateway and collector run with `OMNESIS_EXPERIMENTAL=1`. Connect a Telegram account with your own API app from my.telegram.org, a QR code scanned from Telegram, and the two-step password when the account has one (used once, never stored); the connection survives a collector restart and changes to the source's settings, a session ended from another Telegram device puts the source in needs-auth, and removing the source ends its Telegram session. Private chats, Saved Messages, groups, supergroups and archived chats are archived as one searchable conversation per chat per day; broadcast channels stay out unless you switch them on, and chats you list under Excluded chats are skipped. The whole history is read in the background at a measured pace, through a Telegram data export when Telegram grants one; edits and deletions are followed, messages missed while the collector was offline are recovered, forum messages carry their topic, and self-destructing and view-once messages are never kept. Voice notes are transcribed into the day's conversation, photos and documents become searchable attachments under your attachment settings, and other audio, video and stickers stay placeholders. The local archive and connection cache are encrypted with the collector's new `telegram-store` key.
