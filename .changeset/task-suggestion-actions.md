Experimental task suggestions can be accepted with a note, accepted for a chosen day, month or someday, marked already done, or ignored. A note written while accepting is saved as a Tell Omnesis note about that task and applied to it by the note agent. Ignoring a suggestion only takes it out of Tasks: Omnesis keeps tracking the loop behind it and does not suggest it again. On iOS and Android a suggestion looks like any task, with the actions behind a long press, and the banner announcing suggestions sits at the top of Tasks.
