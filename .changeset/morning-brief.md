Experimental Brain: the Briefs feed is replaced by one morning brief. Brain runs note what you may want to hear about, each morning one run rechecks those notes and writes what still holds into a pinned Morning brief conversation, and nothing is sent when there is nothing to say; replies in that conversation act on the latest brief's items. Brain suggestions appear in Tasks as Suggested until you accept them, and the Brain only proposes changes to tasks you own. It learns what you care about from what you accept, ignore, create or undo in Tasks and from the thumbs on each brief item, and weighs the past reactions most like each new item when it decides what to suggest or mention; `brain.interest.enabled` switches that off. The brief push notification and the `brief-judge` model role are removed.
