WhatsApp and iMessage day chats show each untranscribed voice note's state — awaiting transcription, no transcriber available, no speech detected, audio unavailable, or audio not downloaded on this device — instead of a bare placeholder. The Sources page, the apps, `omnesis sources` and `omnesis members` show how many notes are waiting, and `omnesis doctor` warns when no transcriber is assigned. iMessage retries a voice note after a transcriber outage as WhatsApp does.
