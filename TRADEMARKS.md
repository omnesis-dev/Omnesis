# Trademarks and brand attribution

Omnesis is an open-source personal data aggregator. To identify the third-party services it integrates with, the source packages display each provider's icon next to the provider's name in the UI. We treat this as **nominative use** — the standard pattern used by password managers, email aggregators, and similar OSS tools. We do not claim ownership of these marks, and we do not imply endorsement, partnership, or sponsorship.

All third-party trademarks, service marks, and logos referenced anywhere in this repository or in the running app are property of their respective owners.

### IMAP host visuals

A Fastmail IMAP connection hot-links Fastmail's first-party touch icon. An iCloud Mail connection uses a generic Lucide cloud glyph because Apple's trademark guidelines forbid third-party use of its app icons. Other IMAP hosts use a generic [Lucide](https://lucide.dev) `mail` glyph (ISC-licensed) tinted to the IMAP accent.

## Install-promo marks

The Sources page install cards show the Chrome, Apple and Android marks to identify where the browser extension, the iPhone app and the Android app come from. The portal's content-security policy forbids remote images, so all three are **bundled locally** in `packages/gateway/portal/img/` instead of hot-linked: `chrome-logo.svg` from the [Google Chrome icon file](<https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Google_Chrome_icon_(February_2022).svg>) and `apple-logo.svg` from the [Apple logo file](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Apple_logo_black.svg) on Wikimedia Commons (both PD-textlogo, trademarked), and `android-logo.svg` from the [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) Android glyph (CC0-1.0), filled in Android green. As elsewhere in this file, this is **nominative use** — the marks remain the property of Google LLC and Apple Inc., and their display implies no endorsement, partnership, or sponsorship.
