## Architectural preference

Strongest breadth-aware flagship set: C01, C02, C03, C05, C08, C09, C11, C15, C17, C45. C40 shares substantial evidence with C01 and C03 and is an excellent additional concept. C06, C30, C34 and C42 offer simpler sharply visible reveals; choose among them to reduce recording risk. C04 is useful but derives its final magic from an external routing service. C21 should be selected only after verifying native graph edge types instead of inventing semantic relationships.

Health/privacy, finance reconciliation, contradiction-aware personal memory and actual media processing are the strongest differentiators. The largest architecture risks are fixed fixture dates, false completeness claims, fake processing portrayed as genuine OCR/STT, and group-speaker loss. Good narration can expose uncertainty without weakening the reveal: “Among these covered accounts,” “The booking says X; your actual visit supports Y,” and “This is associated, not a diagnosis.”
