## Privacy and realism assessment

All reviewed identities originate in the independently authored cast. Reserved example-domain addresses and fictional phone ranges are used. Bank institution/platform labels identify public products; balances, account IDs, merchants and narratives are fabricated. These modules do not read production stores, personal configuration, another universe, signing secrets or API credentials. The facts ledger is separate from source fixture directories and `agentDemos` is null. Synthetic API clients still require verification of network override behavior when instantiated; raw-fixture schema checks do not prove that no real request can happen.

The chronology preserves Sacha's September 2016 London arrival, May 2018 meeting with Maya, August 2020 cohabitation and April 2023 move. Background excludes Maya conversations before meeting; historical display names can be current aliases, but original documents must not contain future knowledge. London background excludes known foreign-presence dates. The temporary residence retained during a ten-week placement is not a residence-history gap.

This audit proves coherent portions of static authoring and normalizer compatibility. It does not prove all fifteen runtime scenes, final media, external document writing, route tools, privacy disclosure or Cerebras latency. Those requirements remain separate acceptance gates.
