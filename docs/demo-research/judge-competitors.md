## Recommended set

Ten flagships: **C01, C02, C03, C05, C08, C09, C13, C15, C30, C45**. This covers scanned history, relationships, location, health, audio, warranties, social obligations, finance and governance. Three finance concepts overlap: consolidate C30 into C15 if range is more valuable than two distinct financial reveals; use **C40** as replacement when address-history and absence-history can be filmed with substantially different payoffs.

Five additional supported concepts: **C04, C10, C14, C17, C40**. C04 is a recognisable acquisition hook, C10 has real clinical preparation value, C14 exposes person identity resolution, C17 demonstrates message-only obligations, C40 extends a multi-year document story. C20 is an excellent second angle on C45 and can appear in its recording treatment without becoming a sixteenth concept.

Audit judgment: straightforward retrieval demos are reproducible by competitors when equivalent data is connected. The defensible differentiation is a **verified retained evidence layer that can be reused under explicit governance**, demonstrated through unusually rich reconstruction and meaningful outcomes. Avoid portraying unknown competitor ingestion details as proven data absence.
