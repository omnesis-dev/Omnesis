# Everyday-life demo candidates

Research draft, 3 October 2026. These are candidates, not verified product claims.
Each final selection must have a genuine evidence chain, a visible payoff within
roughly 20–45 seconds, and a runnable prompt against one independently invented
person's corpus. No production corpus is consulted.

Resolve the request's two counts as 15 final video concepts, with 10 flagship
recording scenarios and five additional concepts supported by the same universe.

## Evaluation contract

Independent judges score every candidate from 1 to 5 on clarity, real problem
value, magic/wow, differentiation, and privacy/governance emphasis. Also report
feasibility, unsupported surface claims, minimum evidence, the visible reveal,
and likely false-positive/conflicting-evidence risks. A high score cannot rescue
an unsupported product claim. Diversity and everyday-life value break ties.

## Synthetic cast seed

Proposed owner: Sacha Bellamy, a wholly fictional French software engineer,
aged 30 at the reference date, living in London since 2016. Partner: Maya
Bellamy. Details will be frozen only after scenario selection and an independent
continuity audit. All identities, organisations, conversations and financial
records are invented from scratch. Emails use reserved example domains, phone
numbers use reserved fiction ranges, and street addresses are explicit fictional
placeholders. Real public product names may identify source systems; invented
merchants provide transaction narratives.
