# Personal-agent competitor evidence

Research checked **3 October 2026**. This is a public-source audit, not an account-level functional benchmark. Sources below are vendors' own product/help/security pages, policies, and integration partners' announcements. Product claims are evidence of documented capability, not proof of reliability. **Unknown** means the inspected sources do not settle the question; it must never become “cannot.”

## Defensible demo positioning

1. **Reconstruct a life event from conflicting evidence.** Show a lease period corrected by a dated move conversation and corroborating calendar event. A generic form-filler is competitive; the auditable resolution is the point.
2. **Remember the wish and prove it is still unfulfilled.** Old conversation → expressed interest → search purchase/travel confirmations → eliminate already fulfilled wishes. Do not say Muse cannot access messages.
3. **Explain where and when from independently collected evidence.** Historical visit → tickets → duration → rental. Distinguish measured visits, reservations and inference.
4. **Join quantified data to qualitative context.** Health trend → actual training → travel or voice-note context. Avoid causal/medical claims. Health integration by itself is not unique.
5. **Reuse one governed corpus from multiple agents.** Demonstrate model choice and an independently hosted evidence store, then show scoped disclosure, review and revocation with actual runtime evidence.
6. **Show a deliberate refusal.** A scoped external agent can get an appropriate answer without receiving disallowed personal raw records; prove the policy/audit effect. A refusal alone is common; granular, inspectable control over a reusable corpus is the stronger claim.

No synthetic fixture should contain competitor users' examples, private research screenshots or copied personal stories. All scenario data must be invented independently. These recommendations are research inferences; Omnesis capability and reliability must be proved separately against its current code and live synthetic demonstrations.
