## Narrative invariants

- The itinerary and actual travel can differ; visits establish local presence
  while receipts establish intended travel and paid services.
- Each refund scenario declares posted versus pending rows and exact amounts.
  Separate unrelated similarly priced purchases to prevent shortcut matching.
- A completed gift has positive evidence of attendance/ownership, not just a
  cancelled booking; unfulfilled wishes have no contradictory completion record.
- Sleep changes are associations in a bounded window, not causal or medical
  conclusions. Health/source gaps are disclosed.
- Medical preparation summarises the owner's recorded facts and symptoms,
  without generating a diagnosis or treatment recommendation.
- Tasks marked complete and cancelled events are distinguishable from open
  commitments. Latest corrections override earlier plans with cited evidence.
- Preserve unique order/reference/serial identifiers across message, invoice,
  bank and attachment documents so graph trails have legitimate links.
- Noise is substantial, chronologically plausible and distributed across years,
  people and sources. It contains ordinary successes, cancellations, repayments,
  stale plans, corrections and unanswered conversations—not cloned filler.
- Every flagship has distractors, conflicting evidence where appropriate, and
  a facts ledger linking expected conclusions to exact fixture identifiers.
- No source contains a prepared agent answer or synthetic agent conversation.

## Relative-date contract

Historical residence, marriage, travel, receipt and wish evidence remains fixed.
Near-future tasks, party times and appointment windows are generated from the
recording week's Monday in Europe/London. A materialised universe is immutable
for an instance's lifetime; ordinary restart does not silently move its data.
A fresh load chooses a fresh week. Tests must prove next-week selections on
weekdays, Sunday, year rollover and London daylight-saving boundaries. Prompt
cards state the materialised dates whenever asking about a specific date.
