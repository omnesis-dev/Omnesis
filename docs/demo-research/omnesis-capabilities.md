# Omnesis capabilities for everyday-life demonstrations

This is a code-grounded capability audit, not a claim that an installed gateway has every source connected or every model available. No production gateway, production configuration or personal corpus was accessed for this audit. Paths below refer to repository code. Runtime demonstrations must separately prove retrieval, citations and enabled capabilities against the synthetic gateway.

## What the product can demonstrate

Omnesis can join private evidence that is normally separated between messaging, mail, files, calendar, phone sensors and structured accounts. Its strongest demonstrations answer a concrete life question with several independent pieces of evidence, make conflicts explicit, and show the source citations. It can recover a move date where a tenancy term overlaps another term; distinguish a partner's unfulfilled wish from a purchase already made; relate a health trend to a travel period; or reconcile a cancellation email with a later bank charge. These are reasoning tasks over available evidence, not guaranteed conclusions or new upstream write operations.

## Confirmed surfaces and their boundaries

**Built-in portal/iPhone/Android agent.** Uses the batch retrieval, graph, people, SQL and temporal tools above when their ports are wired. The canonical tool catalogue is `packages/agent/src/tools/registry.ts`. Health, location and finance questions are evidence retrieval and analysis; the product does not turn those answers into clinical, legal or financial guarantees.

**Apple Watch is implemented.** `ios/project.yml` declares watchOS application and complication targets, with deployment target 10.0. `ios/Sources/OmnesisWatch/` contains ask/note intents, dictation, routing, voice capture and outbox handling; `ios/Sources/OmnesisWatchWidgets/` contains complications. The watch owns no gateway pairing or HealthKit permission. `WatchLink.swift` relays through the paired iPhone over WatchConnectivity; the phone owns pairing and gateway access. The watch is therefore a credible surface for a short question and a follow-up after actual device validation. It is not a standalone gateway client or the component collecting Apple Health. Gateway dictation availability is published by the phone; otherwise the watch uses system dictation. Delivery/retry/fallback behavior is in the shared `Intents/WatchRelayDelivery.swift` contract. Deep Research is incompatible with the bounded voice surface (`http/routes/agent.ts`).

**External agents through Direct MCP.** `packages/gateway/src/agent/direct-mcp.ts` exposes stable `search_many`, `fetch_many`, `lookup_document_by_url`, `lookup_people`, `trace_connections`, `run_sql`, `temporal_query` and `list_tables`. The facade is read-only and does not provide document editing, a shell, filesystem writes, upstream email sending or arbitrary admin actions. Restricted source grants have a narrower tool set, excluding cross-corpus people/graph/time surfaces. External agents supply their own reasoning and can use their own tools for final actions.

**External agents through Answer MCP/API.** `packages/gateway/src/privacy/answer-service.ts` runs private retrieval and reviews what can leave under the connection's policy. Answer and Direct are different trust arrangements. An Answer result is reviewed disclosure, while Direct returns authorized retrieval results to the external reasoning loop. Approval-required results can stop a seemingly instant demonstration; choose and disclose a policy appropriate to the fictional corpus, or make the approval itself the demonstration. `privacy/reviewer.ts`, `privacy/direct-session.ts`, `privacy/direct-audit.ts`, and `privacy/policy-schema.ts` implement review, session decisions, audit and policy. A successful built-in question does not by itself prove the external Answer path: its tools, disclosure review and conversation continuity must also be exercised.

**OpenClaw and Hermes.** `packages/agent-integration/` and the corresponding providers implement off-host transcript ingestion, subscription delivery and scoped management surfaces. These integrations can demonstrate Omnesis as private context behind a different assistant, but the assistant's sending, scheduling or purchasing tools remain separate capabilities.

**DOCX output is an external-agent action.** `extract-office.ts` implements DOCX input extraction. The built-in tool registry has no file-authoring/DOCX-editing tool. `packages/agent/src/backend.ts` and `packages/agent/src/session.ts` persist a Deep Research `report_artifact` alongside text and citations; that artifact is not a generated Word file. A residence-history demonstration should use Claude Code/Cowork or another assistant with a real document-writing tool: Omnesis retrieves the evidence, that assistant fills the supplied DOCX, and the output is opened and checked. Do not describe the Word-file write as an Omnesis-native feature.

**External world facts remain external.** There is no routing, traffic or shopping tool in the builtin registry. For “when should I leave?”, Omnesis can retrieve the party time/address and contextual messages; ChatGPT or another host uses its own maps/current-traffic tool to compute travel. Price, availability, opening times and fresh public information require the host's independent external tools. Omnesis evidence alone cannot establish them.

The newly added twins have passed fixture schema/mapping checks; source registration, real-reader isolation and end-to-end ingestion must still be proved. See `continuity-review.md`. This table is a source inventory, not permission to silently drop the untwinned sources from a requested “everything” universe. Represent them using a safe fictional ingestion seam or add the missing synthetic support. Verify registered source IDs, the device owner, document/row counts and source-specific semantics after loading. A twin that spreads a real descriptor must override real authentication, cleanup and context methods; `providers-synth/google/src/index.ts` shows those overrides explicitly.

**Plaid and Bank of America.** Plaid is a real provider, not an invented demo-only type. `packages/providers/plaid/src/provider.ts` runs hosted Plaid Link; `index.ts` declares the source; `schemas.ts` declares `plaid_transactions`, `plaid_balances` and `plaid_holdings`; `sync.ts` handles ingestion. One bank connection is a Plaid item. Institution branding is loaded separately and failure leaves a generic Plaid icon. A Bank of America-branded synthetic item may carry fictional transactions without contacting the bank. This audit does not prove current institution availability or credentials in any live Plaid environment; do not promise that every bank/account/product is supported. The demo must not reuse real bank account numbers or credentials.

**Apple Health and Strava are distinct feeds.** `ios/Sources/Omnesis/AppleHealth/AppleHealthSource.swift` is the real iPhone HealthKit source. Its synthetic counterpart declares health schemas and maps rows in `providers-synth/apple-health/src/`. Strava's `schemas.ts` includes activities, splits, best efforts, laps, segment efforts, zones, comments, kudos, streams, athlete metadata/stats and gear. Decide which stream owns a measurement and avoid double-counting a workout appearing in both. Test the actual synthetic tables available: a broad real-provider schema does not guarantee a synthetic fixture populates every table.

**Location is named visit history, not continuous GPS.** `ios/Sources/Omnesis/CoreLocationVisits/CoreLocationVisitsSource.swift` indexes completed dwells after on-device reverse geocoding; uncompleted or unnamed visits are skipped for later retry. Searchable content uses place names. `PlacesSetupStep.swift` handles permission, with Always/full access as the complete authorization. The source is partitioned by phone. It cannot reconstruct every route, every precise position, or visits before monitoring started. A train booking indicates intended travel; a completed location visit corroborates presence. The synthetic twin emits both documents and `location_visits` analytics. Preserve place, arrival/departure, timezone and transport chronology consistently.

## Gates, privacy and operational limits

- Stable: ordinary chat, explicit Deep Research, standard Answer/Direct, quick capture, voice ask, dictation when transcription is runnable, grounded interactive memory, graph retrieval, SQL and `temporal_query`.
- Experimental: autonomous Brain/annotation generation, Briefs, Sweeps, open-loop/cognitive context tools and Watches. `OMNESIS_EXPERIMENTAL=1` and `experimentalVisible()` control discovery; synthetic mode can expose experimental surfaces. A synthetic demo must identify the mode rather than accidentally advertising an experimental workflow as a stable default. Tool gates are explicit in `packages/agent/src/tools/registry.ts`; Direct has separate stable/restricted allowlists.
- Apple Watch is a device; experimental Watches are automation rules. They are unrelated features despite the shared word.
- Local corpus storage and local audio transcription do not imply all inference is local. `ocr/loader.ts` can select Codex or HTTP vision OCR; chat/completion assignments can use remote inference. A Cerebras-powered demo sends model context to the chosen backend. Claims should distinguish local storage, source control, scoped external disclosure, and inference routing.
- Source selection, device pairing, authorization scope and policy are real boundaries, not cosmetic controls. A read grant should not be described as permission to modify external files, send messages or move money.
- Exclusions, deletion and revocation should be demonstrated through supported APIs. Never improvise a privacy showcase by accessing a production corpus. Fixtures, names, identifiers and stories must be independently invented.
- Accuracy depends on coverage and freshness. Missing source accounts, failed syncs, omitted historical messages, capped extraction, truncated SQL/time results and stale index/enrichment can all produce plausible incomplete answers. An absence of evidence is not evidence that a gift was never bought or a charge never refunded.

## Implications for a genuine live-agent universe

A successful universe must contain enough unrelated background material that the agent retrieves a needle, rather than choosing from a handful of obvious scenario documents. Relevant evidence should disagree realistically in at least selected scenarios, with the resolution grounded in an additional source rather than a hidden answer key. Do not put the expected conclusion into document titles, tags or synthetic metadata used by retrieval.

The corpus should exercise exact IDs and links for graph traversal, deterministic temporal projections for calendar/tasks, text date mentions for contracts and messages, row-level analytics for finance/health, and actual binary ingestion for at least the OCR/transcription demonstrations. Matching prose across sources is useful semantic evidence but not a substitute for an explicit attachment/thread/URL edge.

Fixture providers commonly normalize prewritten text directly; that path alone does not prove PDF OCR or voice transcription. Run at least one real generated scanned PDF through a runnable OCR backend and one independently generated audio clip through local transcription, retaining the resulting searchable document and provenance. Keep these separate from model-inference-free unit/E2E tests; an operator-authorized live-agent rehearsal is a distinct validation exercise.

Relative dates must be materialized coherently across all records, documents, embedded date text and binary assets. Existing universe selection in `providers-synth/_common/src/universe.ts` is not itself a date-shifting guarantee. Confirm “next week” after restarting the demo with a different anchor. Historical ten-year residence evidence can stay fixed; use an explicit policy that preserves ages, durations, calendar weekday alignment and travel relationships where relevant. An internal generator answer key should never be ingestible by the agent.

Cerebras speed must be measured on the final dataset and tool loop. Batched tools reduce round trips, but indexing, enrichment, embedding, OCR/transcription, SQL joins and deep-research fanout still affect elapsed time. Do not sell a replay as a live response. No synthetic replay agent conversations belong in this universe; the evidence must support fresh model reasoning.

Each selected demonstration needs an evidence checklist, actual prompt, authoritative assertions and a genuine rehearsal on the loaded gateway. For external demonstrations also exercise Answer/Direct grants, review behavior and the host's separate action tool. For native watch filming, separately prove relay and follow-up on the watch/phone pair. Searchability alone cannot certify the final scene.
